## Reflection<br>
**What I learned across all modules:**<br>
This course took me through the full machine learning workflow, from understanding data to building, evaluating, and explaining models. I started with the fundamentals of machine learning, then learned classification and how to evaluate models with precision, recall, F1-score, ROC curves, and confusion matrices. I explored Support Vector Machines and the importance of feature scaling and kernels, and Decision Trees and how to control overfitting. In dimensionality reduction, I learned that PCA can greatly speed up some models, but not others, and that t-SNE is a powerful tool for visualizing high-dimensional data. I then built neural networks with TensorFlow and Keras, using early stopping, checkpoints, and TensorBoard, and finished with natural language processing by building an SMS spam classifier that reached about 98% accuracy.<br>

**Skills I developed:**<br>
- Data loading, exploration, and preprocessing (scaling, train/validation/test splits, text cleaning, tokenization, and stopword removal)<br>
- Model building with Scikit-Learn (classification, SVMs, Decision Trees, Random Forests) and TensorFlow/Keras (multilayer perceptrons)<br>
- Model tuning and evaluation (learning rate search, early stopping, accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, confusion matrices)<br>
- Dimensionality reduction and text representation (PCA, t-SNE, Bag-of-Words with CountVectorizer)<br>
- Visualization and communication (Matplotlib charts, TensorBoard, Graphviz flowcharts, interactive widgets)<br>
- Working in Google Colab and using Generative AI responsibly for coding and debugging<br>

**How these projects prepare me for real-world ML tasks:**<br>
Each project followed the same steps used in industry: understand the business problem, prepare the data, build and train a model, evaluate it honestly, and explain the results. These projects taught me to measure results instead of assuming them, to choose the right metric when data is imbalanced, and to communicate findings clearly to non-technical audiences. Projects like the SMS spam classifier directly reflect real business applications such as fraud detection, customer support automation, and content filtering, preparing me to build and evaluate practical machine learning solutions.<br>

## AI Statement<br>
I used Generative AI tools, including Claude and Gemini in Google Colab, throughout these projects as a learning and productivity assistant. I used AI to help write and explain code, debug errors such as missing imports, slow-running cells, a TensorBoard extension that would not load, and a missing Graphviz installation, and to draft clear explanations of concepts like PCA, Random Forests, Keras, TensorBoard, tokenization, and Bag-of-Words. I did not accept AI output blindly: I ran every cell myself, compared AI-generated explanations against my own results, and corrected mistakes when the AI was wrong. Using AI this way helped me learn faster while making sure I understood every step and took full responsibility for my work.<br>
